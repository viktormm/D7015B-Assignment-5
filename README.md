## Task 1: Ground Level

We find the ground levels by analyzing the z-value (on x-axis) where we have the most points (count on y-axis) on the histogram. 
This is because LiDAR captures most points on ground level and we can therefore find at what z (height) these are by identifying in what bin we have by far
the highest amount of observered values.
For Dataset 1: Ground level = 61.250
For Dataset 2: Ground level = 61.265
